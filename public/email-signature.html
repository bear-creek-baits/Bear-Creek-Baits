Put it in Gmail (on a computer)

  1. In Gmail, click the gear (top right), then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll down to Signature and click Create new. Name it Bear Creek Baits.
  3. Click in the big box and paste (Ctrl+V).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick it for both new emails and on reply/forward.
  5. Tick Insert signature before quoted text in replies, then scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

The Gmail phone app uses its own plain-text signature (in the app’s settings). Emails sent from a computer get this one.
